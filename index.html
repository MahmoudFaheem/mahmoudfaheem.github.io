Mahmoud Faheem

Graduate student with training in bioinformatics, microbial genomics, metagenomics, and antimicrobial resistance analysis

I am a microbiologist and bioinformatician with academic training in pharmaceutical sciences, genomics, and computational biology. My research interests focus on microbial genomics, antimicrobial resistance, and the application of bioinformatics to understanding microbial adaptation and resistance mechanisms.

I currently serve as a Teaching Assistant in the Department of Microbiology & Immunology at Modern University for Technology & Information (MTI) while pursuing an MSc in Pharmaceutical Sciences at Ain Shams University. My work spans molecular microbiology, sequence analysis, resistome profiling, and scientific database development.

Antimicrobial Resistance Genes Database

COCTAIL – COVID-19 Clinical Trial and Intervention Literature Hub

An evidence-based literature repository mapping over 1,900 PubMed-indexed COVID-19 clinical and epidemiological trials through systematic text mining and manual curation. Adopted and hosted on the Faculty of Pharmacy, Cairo University institutional portal and presented at ISCB 2021.

03 — Competencies

Technical Skills

Bioinformatics & Genomics

Sequence alignment, metagenomic and genomic read processing (FASTQ/FASTA), resistome profiling, variant annotation, and database queries across NCBI, PubMed, BLAST, CARD, BV-BRC, and Ensembl.

Programming & Scripting

R (RStudio), Python, Bash scripting (Linux/Unix), and AWK for data processing and statistical visualization; introductory experience with SQL and PHP.

Computational Tools

Command-line sequence analysis and alignment workflows using Bowtie2, DIAMOND, SAMtools, BEDTools, and the NCBI SRA Toolkit.

Molecular & Bench Microbiology

BSL-2 aseptic practices, bacterial isolation and culture, Gram/differential staining, biochemical identification, antimicrobial susceptibility testing (disc diffusion & MIC), genomic DNA extraction (QIAamp), PCR, agarose gel electrophoresis, and MCF-7 cell viability assays (MTT, Trypan blue).

Independent Application

FASTQ/FASTA processing, Bowtie2, DIAMOND, SAMtools, BEDTools, NCBI SRA Toolkit, R, Python, Bash, AWK, BSL-2 microbiology, AST (MIC & disc diffusion), DNA extraction, PCR, gel electrophoresis.

Supervised Research Experience

MCF-7 mammalian cell culture maintenance, hemocytometer cell counting, Trypan blue exclusion, and MTT colorimetric cytotoxicity assays.

Introductory / Working Knowledge

Relational database design with SQL and backend web integration with PHP for biological repositories.
