Literature Mining & Clinical Trial Curation • 2020 – Present (Published Institutional Resource)

COCTAIL – COVID-19 Clinical Trial and Intervention Literature Hub

An evidence-based literature repository mapping PubMed-indexed COVID-19 clinical and epidemiological trials.

The Host-Pathogen Network ↗ COCTAIL Mirror ↗

1. Scientific Background

During the rapid expansion of COVID-19 clinical and epidemiological literature, clinicians and researchers required a structured, verifiable index separating primary clinical trial reports from commentary and secondary reviews.

2. Objectives

  • Retrieve, filter, and systematically index PubMed-registered COVID-19 clinical and epidemiological trials.
  • Classify therapeutic interventions, trial designs, and publication metadata into an accessible public repository.
  • Provide a public reference portal for academic and clinical use.

3. Specific Contribution

Developed and maintained an evidence-based database of PubMed-indexed COVID-19 clinical and epidemiological studies.

4. Methodology

Over 150,000 PubMed records were manually screened to identify relevant studies, extract key information, and build a curated database.

5. Key Outputs

  • Mapped and indexed over 1,900 PubMed-indexed COVID-19 clinical and epidemiological trials.
  • Deployed as an official institutional resource on the Faculty of Pharmacy, Cairo University website (COCTAIL Mirror ↗).

7. Related Presentations

Presented as a poster: Text Mining – COCTAIL: The COVID-19 Clinical Trial and Intervention Literature Hub (Prof. Ramy K. Aziz, Mahmoud Faheem, Shaimaa Hegazy) at the International Society for Computational Biology (ISCB) Conference, Colorado, USA, December 2021.
