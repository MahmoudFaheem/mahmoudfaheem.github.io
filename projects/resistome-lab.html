Resistome-Lab Computational Resources

Open-source bioinformatics workflows, sequence analysis scripts, and statistical tools for microbial genomics and resistome profiling.

github.com/Resistome-lab ↗ Personal GitHub ↗

1. Scientific Background & Research Question

Quantifying antimicrobial resistance determinants and evaluating their mobility potential across bacterial genomes and complex metagenomes requires standardized, reproducible command-line workflows rather than ad-hoc manual steps.

2. Objectives

  • Develop modular command-line pipelines for read-based and assembly-based resistome profiling.
  • Automate mobile genetic element (MGE) signature detection and quantitative mobility risk assessment on genomic contigs.
  • Provide reproducible statistical visualization scripts in R and Python for microbiome and resistome datasets.

3. Specific Contribution

Founder and Lead Developer of the Resistome-Lab GitHub organization. Architecting command-line utilities, HMM signature screening workflows, and downstream statistical analysis scripts.

4. Methodology & Technologies

Built around Unix/Linux command-line environments combining Python, R, Bash, and AWK with standard bioinformatics engines including Bowtie2, DIAMOND, SAMtools, BEDTools, and CARD reference databases.

5. Key Outputs & Results

  • Publicly accessible repositories under github.com/Resistome-lab supporting contig mobility scoring, resistome mapping, and statistical reporting.
  • Reusable command-line templates for reproducible microbial genomics analysis.

6. System Architecture & Data Flow

[Raw Sequencing Reads / Contigs (FASTQ/FASTA)] → [Alignment & Profiling (Bowtie2 / DIAMOND / CARD)] → [MGE & Mobility Scoring] → [Statistical Analysis & Visualization (R / Python)]

7. Related Presentations & Scholarship

Supports ongoing MSc thesis research in microbial genomics and resistome profiling at the Faculty of Pharmacy, Ain Shams University.
